# Multinex profile

Use the shared core with optional MemQ workflows for durable knowledge and realm-bound coordination. Keep project languages, tools and deployment choices in their owning repositories.

Coordinator, builder, researcher and reviewer are responsibilities, not privileged identities. An authorized agent may take a role when its actual capabilities fit the task. Delegation remains optional and subject to host and user authorization.

Keep organization-specific account mappings, client policies, role aliases and engagement metadata in private local profiles. They are not required to use this public package.
